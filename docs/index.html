Tiny GPT From Scratch

A from-scratch transformer, trained and improved, written only in NumPy.

A small GPT written in pure NumPy across config.py, model.py, and scaffold.py, trained on the Karpathy Tiny Shakespeare corpus with a 30-character vocabulary.

The architecture

The model is a 6-layer decoder-only transformer with 8 attention heads, a model dimension of 192, and a feed-forward width of 768. That works out to roughly 1.5 million parameters. Layers are pre-norm with residual connections on the outside, and the output head is tied to the token embedding. The context window is 128 tokens.

Training

Trained with Adam at batch size 16, a 200-step warmup, cosine decay down to a floor of 3e-5, weight decay of 0.1, and gradient clipping at 1.0. The baseline model trained for 4,000 steps and reached a validation loss of 1.4587 on a 64-token context.

The improvement pass

The baseline checkpoint was warm-started: weights and Adam moments carried over, the learning rate schedule reset, and the context widened from 64 to 128 by tiling the learned positional rows into the new half. After 1,000 more steps the improved checkpoint scores 1.5248 at block 128 and 1.4450 at block 64.

Sampling

Text is generated with top-k sampling at k=30 with temperature tempering.
